Fuel & Wrench is the logbook for cars and motorcycles: fuel-ups, consumption, service history and tyres in one place – no ads, no tracking, no Google services.

<b>Fuel & consumption</b>
• Fuel-ups with fuel type, station, location, driving style, route, tyres and extra consumers
• Full-to-full consumption, cost per 100 km, consumption chart
• Per-vehicle units: km/miles, litres/gallons, l/100 km, km/l or mpg, any currency
• Fuel stations on a map (OpenStreetMap)

<b>Service & reminders</b>
• Service history timeline with costs and workshop
• Mileage-based maintenance reminders, created automatically
• Wheel sets (summer/winter), wheel changes with tread depth at storage, different tyres per axle

<b>Import & sync</b>
• Import Spritmonitor.de exports and service histories (CSV)
• Sync via your own Nextcloud / WebDAV – multiple devices at once

<b>Privacy</b>
All data stays on your device or your own server. Online services can be switched off individually.
